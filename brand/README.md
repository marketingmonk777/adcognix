# AdCognix brand kit
- logo/svg: mark, horizontal logo (light, dark, mono), stacked logo, wordmark. SVG for print and web; PNG in multiple sizes.
- app-icons: iOS 1024, Android 512, adaptive foreground and background, PWA maskable, splash.
- favicon: .ico, .svg, PNG sizes, apple-touch-icon.
- social: LinkedIn, Facebook, X, YouTube covers, OG image, profile avatar; templates for Instagram post and story and LinkedIn post.
- stationery: business card front and back (3.5x2 in at 300 dpi), A4 letterhead (SVG and PDF), email signature HTML.
- fonts: Manrope (headings), Inter (body). Both are free under the SIL Open Font License.
