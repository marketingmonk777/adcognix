About AdCognix

Advertising that a busy owner can actually run.

Most small businesses know their customers are on Instagram, Facebook and Google. What they lack is the time to learn three ad managers. AdCognix puts planning, creative, publishing and follow-up into one simple app.

What we believe

Clarity over jargon. Every screen explains what a setting does in plain words, and every suggestion says why.

Your money, your decision. AdCognix suggests budgets and changes, but nothing goes live or moves without your approval.

Honest numbers. We show results from your own campaigns. Where a screen uses example data, it says so.

Leads are yours. Export them any time. We never sell your data or your customers' data.
